Built like a real quad

  • FPV, line of sight and chase

    Fly through the goggles, from the pilot spot like at the field, or with a camera behind the quad. Switch any time.

  • Betaflight-style rates

    RC rate, super rate and expo per axis with live curves. Acro or Angle mode, throttle expo and limit.

  • Gamepad, touch or keyboard

    Plug in a gamepad or USB radio (Modes 1–4, axis setup), fly with on-screen sticks on a phone or tablet, or use the keyboard.

  • Meta Quest VR

    Stand on the pilot platform and fly with Touch controllers, with altitude hold on the thumbsticks.
