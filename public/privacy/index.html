Privacy Policy

Drone Sim · COWORK Game Studio · Effective 30 September 2026

Summary: Drone Sim does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Who we are

Drone Sim ("the app") is an FPV drone flight simulator published by COWORK Game Studio ("we", "us") for iPhone, iPad, Android, Meta Quest and web browsers at dronesim.coworkgamestudio.com.

Data we collect

None. The app makes no network requests while you play: it contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or social SDKs, and it does not ask you to sign in.

Data stored on your device

To remember your preferences and records, the app saves a few values in the device's local storage. They never leave your device and are not readable by us.

WhatWhy
Settings (controls, stick mode, rates, camera, graphics quality, volume, heading arrow)Keep your setup between sessions
Best race time and ring split timesShow your personal best and split deltas
Whether you dismissed the "Add to Home Screen" tip (web only)Not show the tip again

You can erase this data at any time by deleting the app, or on the web by clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

Device features the app uses

  • Touch, keyboard and game controllers — read only to fly the drone, processed on the device.
  • Meta Quest headset and Touch controllers (VR mode) — head and controller position are used only to draw the view and fly the drone in real time. They are not recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Audio output and vibration — for motor sound and rumble feedback. The app does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play, Meta Horizon Store) and the web host (GitHub Pages) may process data such as downloads, purchases or server logs under their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal data from them beyond aggregated, anonymous store statistics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
